# Problem Statement

## Project Title

**InternMatch – Virtual Internship Platform for Students**

## 1. Domain

**Virtual Internship Platform for Students**

## 2. Problem Overview

Students often face difficulty in finding suitable internship opportunities and managing their internship activities in a structured manner. Internship information can be scattered across different platforms, making it difficult for students to discover relevant opportunities, apply for them, and keep track of their internship-related activities.

A centralized virtual internship platform can simplify this process by providing students with a single platform to discover internships, apply for opportunities, complete assigned internship activities, and track their progress.

## 3. Problem Statement

The **InternMatch** project aims to develop a web-based virtual internship platform that helps students discover and manage internship opportunities through a centralized system.

The platform is designed to support the complete student internship journey, starting from discovering suitable internships and applying for opportunities to completing internship-related tasks and tracking progress.

The project focuses on providing a simple, structured, and user-friendly platform that allows students to manage their internship activities efficiently.

## 4. Objectives

The main objectives of InternMatch are:

* To provide students with a centralized platform for discovering internship opportunities.
* To allow students to search and browse available internships.
* To allow students to apply for and withdraw from internship opportunities.
* To allow students to save internship opportunities for future reference.
* To provide a dashboard for viewing internship-related activities and progress.
* To support the management of internship-related tasks and activities as the platform evolves.
* To help students track their internship progress in a structured manner.
* To provide a foundation for intelligent internship recommendations and performance tracking.

## 5. Target Users

The primary target users of InternMatch are:

* **Students** looking for internship opportunities.
* **Students** who want to manage their applications and internship activities.
* **Students** who want to track their internship progress.

## 6. Core MVP Features

The current MVP focuses on the essential student-side internship discovery and application workflow.

### 6.1 User Registration

Students can create an account by providing the required registration details.

### 6.2 User Login

Registered users can log in to access the InternMatch platform.

### 6.3 Dashboard

The dashboard provides an overview of the student's internship activity, including:

* Available internships
* Applied internships
* Saved internships

### 6.4 Internship Listing

Students can view available internship opportunities with relevant details such as:

* Internship title
* Company
* Location
* Stipend or pay information

### 6.5 Internship Search

Students can search available internship opportunities to identify relevant internships more easily.

### 6.6 Apply / Unapply

Students can apply for an internship opportunity and withdraw their application when required.

### 6.7 Save / Unsave

Students can save internship opportunities for later reference and remove them from their saved list when no longer required.

### 6.8 Profile

Students can view their basic profile information.

### 6.9 Logout

Students can securely log out of the platform after completing their activities.

## 7. Planned Full-Product Capabilities

The complete product vision of InternMatch extends beyond the current MVP and is intended to support:

* Internship application management
* Internship task workflows
* Task completion tracking
* Internship progress tracking
* Student performance tracking
* Intelligent internship recommendations

For the **AI & Data Science specialization**, the platform can be extended with AI/DS-based capabilities such as internship recommendations and performance analysis based on relevant student and internship data.

These capabilities represent the broader product direction and are not claimed as completed features of the current MVP unless implemented.

## 8. Scope of the Current MVP

The current MVP is intentionally focused on establishing the core student-side workflow:

**Home → Sign Up → Login → Dashboard → Browse Internships → Search → Apply / Unapply → Save / Unsave → Profile → Logout**

The MVP demonstrates the fundamental internship discovery and application management experience.

Advanced capabilities such as task workflows, detailed progress tracking, AI-based recommendations, and performance analytics are part of the broader product scope and can be implemented in subsequent development stages.

## 9. Expected Outcome

The expected outcome of InternMatch is a functional virtual internship platform that provides students with a centralized and structured way to discover and manage internship opportunities.

The current MVP demonstrates the essential student workflow, while the overall product architecture provides a foundation for extending the system with internship tasks, progress tracking, performance analysis, and intelligent recommendations.

## 10. Project Limitations

The current MVP does not yet implement all capabilities of the complete virtual internship platform.

The following features are outside the current MVP implementation:

* Employer-side internship management
* Internship task assignment and submission workflows
* Detailed internship progress tracking
* Advanced student performance analytics
* AI-based internship recommendation
* Notifications and reminders
* Interview scheduling
* Payment processing
* Production-scale cloud deployment

These features may be considered for future development based on project requirements and available development scope.

## 11. Success Criteria

The current MVP will be considered successful when:

* A student can create an account and log in.
* A logged-in student can access the dashboard.
* Available internships can be viewed and searched.
* A student can apply for and unapply from an internship.
* A student can save and unsave an internship.
* Dashboard information reflects the student's internship activity.
* A student can access their profile.
* A student can log out successfully.
* The complete core student workflow can be demonstrated from start to finish.

The broader product will ultimately aim to support internship task management, progress tracking, performance analysis, and intelligent internship recommendations.
